## 1. Project Objective
To select a standardized laptop model for the company's general staff (Finance, HR, Admin). Standardizing hardware ensures faster IT support, compatible spare parts, and better bulk pricing.

## 2. Market Comparison (Business-Grade Laptops)

| Feature | Dell Latitude 3450 | Lenovo ThinkPad E14 | HP ProBook 440 G10 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Processor** | Intel Core i5 | Intel Core i5 | Intel Core i5 |
| **RAM / SSD** | 16GB / 512GB | 16GB / 512GB | 16GB / 512GB |
| **OS Version** | **Windows 11 Pro** | **Windows 11 Pro** | **Windows 11 Pro** |
| **Warranty** | **3-Yr ProSupport** | 1-Yr Premier | 1-Yr On-site |
| **Unit Price** | RM 3,850 | **RM 3,450** | RM 3,600 |

## 3. Executive Recommendation
**Selected Model:** **Dell Latitude 3450**

**Reasoning:**
Even though the Lenovo is **RM 400 cheaper**, I recommend the Dell for two key business reasons:
1. **Next Business Day (NBD) Support:** Dell’s ProSupport means if a laptop breaks, a technician comes to our office the next day. This minimizes staff downtime.
2. **Windows 11 Pro:** Essential for joining the Company Domain (Active Directory/Azure) and enabling BitLocker encryption.

## 4. Procurement Impact
* **Total Budget (10 Units):** RM 38,500.
* **Cost Saving:** By selecting a single vendor, we can negotiate a **5% bulk discount**, saving the company RM 1,925.
* **Management:** All 10 units will use the same driver set, reducing setup time by 50%.
