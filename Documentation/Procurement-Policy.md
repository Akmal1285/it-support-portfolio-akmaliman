# IT Procurement Policy & Procedures

## 1. Approval Thresholds
To ensure budget control, all IT purchases follow these limits:
- **Under RM 500:** IT Executive approval only (e.g., mice, cables).
- **RM 501 - RM 5,000:** HOD + IT Executive approval (e.g., standard laptops).
- **Above RM 5,000:** CFO / Director approval required (e.g., Servers, bulk orders).

## 2. The 3-Quote Rule
For any purchase above **RM 1,000**, the IT Executive must source at least **three (3) formal quotations** from different vendors to ensure market-competitive pricing.

## 3. Standardized Vendor List
- **Hardware:** Dell Malaysia, HP Authorized Resellers.
- **Software:** Microsoft (M365), Adobe.
- **Consumables:** Local IT Supply Shops.
