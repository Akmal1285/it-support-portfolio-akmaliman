# IT Executive Case Study: Business Fiber ISP Selection (2025)

## 1. Project Objective
To evaluate and select the most reliable and cost-effective High-Speed Business Broadband (300Mbps) for the office HQ, ensuring 99.9% uptime for cloud-based operations and secure remote VPN access.

## 2. Competitive Matrix (300Mbps Business Plans)

| Feature | TM UniFi Business | TIME dotCom | Maxis Business Fibre |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monthly Fee** | RM 139 (Promo) | **RM 128** | RM 139 (Promo) |
| **Upload Speed** | 50 Mbps | **300 Mbps (Symmetrical)** | 50 Mbps |
| **Fixed IP (1 IP)** | + RM 200/mth | + RM 200/mth | + RM 200/mth |
| **SLA / Backup** | 24-Hr Restore | 99.9% Uptime SLA | **4G Wireless Backup** |
| **Contract** | 24 Months | 24 Months | 24 Months |

## 3. Technical Justification
As the IT Executive, I have analyzed the following requirements for the business:

* **Symmetrical Speed:** **TIME dotCom** is recommended for roles requiring heavy cloud uploads (backups, video calls), as it offers 300Mbps upload, whereas competitors are capped at 50Mbps.
* **Redundancy:** **Maxis** is the preferred alternative if the office building has frequent fiber cable cuts, due to their integrated 4G backup dongle.
* **Static IP Necessity:** A **Fixed IP (RM 200/mth)** is included in the final recommendation to support the company’s Site-to-Site VPN and CCTV remote monitoring.

## 4. Final Recommendation & Budget
**Selected Vendor:** **TIME dotCom (300Mbps) + 1 Fixed IP**
* **Monthly OpEx:** RM 328 (Excluding SST)
* **Annual Cost:** RM 3,936
* **Next Step:** Verify building coverage and initiate **Purchase Order (PO)** for Finance approval.
